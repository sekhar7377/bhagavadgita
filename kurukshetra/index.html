CHAPTER 1 · ARJUNA VIṢĀDA YOGATHE CONCHES SOUND

Across the field,
conch answers conch.
The sound fills earth and sky,
and both armies are ready.

THE FIELD OF QUESTIONSPROLOGUE / KURUKṢETRA
I.

Before the battle begins,
a warrior lays down his bow.
A conversation changes
the way he sees.

THE MOMENT BEFORE

Two armies stand ready.
Arjuna sees teachers, family, friends.
What once seemed certain no longer is.

The Bhagavad Gītā begins with this uncertainty. In the space before action, Arjuna turns to Krishna — and a dialogue unfolds about duty, the self, and how to live.

CHAPTER 2 · SĀṄKHYA YOGATHE TEACHING BEGINS

What is born will pass.
What sees it does not.
Krishna begins with the Self,
and only then the battle.

CHAPTER 6 · DHYĀNA YOGATHE STEADY LAMP

A mind held steady
is a flame where no wind moves.
Attention, practised,
becomes a kind of freedom.

CHAPTER 11 · VIŚVARŪPA DARŚANA YOGATHE UNIVERSAL FORM

Arjuna asks to see.
What he sees has no edge.
Every face, every world,
held in a single form.

CHAPTER 18 · MOKṢA SANNYĀSA YOGACLARITY

The field is the same.
His understanding has changed.
He lifts the bow, not in anger,
but with a clear mind.

A MOMENT TO CONSIDER

What does it mean
to act with clarity?

Not every question asks for an immediate answer.
Some ask us to look again.
